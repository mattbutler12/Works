About

I'm a journalist and editor who spent nearly a decade in local news in Ithaca, New York — rising from general assignment reporter to Editor-in-Chief of a non-profit digital outlet.

My reporting has focused on public health and education, with earlier general-assignment work across city government, courts, and breaking news. As an editor I led newsroom operations, story assignments, and editorial standards at two Ithaca publications.

I'm now seeking my next role — open to reporting, editing, and editorial leadership positions in local or regional news.
